01 / How to use it

A spreadsheet is your decision log.

A CSSBuy spreadsheet is a structured shortlist of product URLs and buying notes. A useful row keeps the original listing, exact variant, size evidence and cost assumptions together. A catalogue helps you discover an item; your saved row explains why you selected it.

STEP 01

Capture the original listing

Open the source link and match the title, colour, size and seller. Save the date checked. If a link fails or the variant differs, pause rather than substituting a similar item.

STEP 02

Review warehouse evidence

Record the QC-photo reference and compare visible details with your order. Request measurements when fit matters. Mark uncertain items as needing review before dispatch.

STEP 03

Reprice the packed parcel

Use the final package dimensions, weight and available route quote. Packaging choices can change the billable weight. Review destination restrictions and return deadlines before committing.

02 / Your comparison template

Keep facts separate from estimates.

The downloadable CSV is a blank planning tool for Excel or Google Sheets. It contains no product endorsements. Add one row per exact product variant and keep each currency explicit.

04 / QC review

Look for evidence you can act on.

  • Match the item, quantity, colour and size against the order.
  • Check visible seams, marks, fasteners and damage in the supplied photos.
  • Ask for specific measurements or a clearer view when the evidence is incomplete.
  • Save the decision and check the applicable return deadline before shipping.

Photos cannot establish every material property, authenticity claim or long-term durability. Treat missing evidence as an open question.

05 / Common questions

Before you order.

Is this an official CSSBuy spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent practical guide. Confirm current service terms directly with CSSBuy.

Where can I discover product links?

The FashionReps CSSBuy catalogue provides a starting point for browsing. Open and verify the original listing, variant and current price before purchasing. A catalogue entry is not a quality guarantee.

Can I use the template in Google Sheets?

Yes. Download the CSV and import it into a new sheet. Keep URLs as text, label currencies, and add your own formulas if needed.

Why does the delivered cost differ from the listed price?

Domestic delivery, agent or payment fees, packing, international freight and destination charges may be separate. The calculator only totals the values you supply.

Does a spreadsheet link guarantee availability?

No. Sellers can change variants, prices and inventory. Recheck the exact source listing and ask the agent about unsupported links before paying.
